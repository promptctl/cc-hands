Every epic in lit's rank order, top first, which is the order its work is pulled in. The line under a title is hands' one-sentence summary; an epic also says how many open tickets it holds, and a tap or click on it shows them, another hides them. To rank an epic or a ticket, drag its card up or down among the cards beside it: by the ⠿ grip on a phone, or from anywhere on the card with a mouse. High, Normal, and Low move a ticket into the epic of its epic's topic for that level, such as "wake word - high", made the first time it is needed; a press shows at once and the page stays still, so go down the list pressing, then press Reload at the top to see the tickets in their new epics and rank those epics like any other. A note under a ticket, saved with Save or ⌘/Ctrl+Enter, is kept by lit as a comment on it, for whoever changes the ticket next.
